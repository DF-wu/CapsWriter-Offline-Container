# Qwen3-ASR quantization and RX570 research

> [Documentation home](README.md) · [繁體中文](../zh-TW/rx570-research-20261115.md) · English

The complete [English scientific report](../reports/rx570-qwen3-asr-research-20261115.md) archives the November 10–15 quantization, community gfx803, Live validation and installed-system investigation. It includes six static figures, methods, failed attempts, numerical and transcript analyses, source references and limitations.

Read the [evidence and regeneration guide](../reports/rx570-20261115/README.md), or use the self-contained [HTML](../reports/rx570-20261115/report.html) and [PDF](../reports/rx570-20261115/report.pdf) reading editions. The Traditional Chinese page is a faithful reading edition of the report, with historical context and links to exhaustive English appendices.

The installed results support CPU encoder plus Vulkan decoder among the tested pairings. MIGraphX demonstrated strict GPU execution but failed the numerical criterion and exhibited shape-dependent stalls. This is a research archive, not a deployment change or broad accuracy certification.
